blurb: Stranger in a Strange Land is a 1961 science fiction novel by American author
  Robert A. Heinlein. It tells the story of Valentine Michael Smith, a human who comes
  to Earth in early adulthood after being born on the planet Mars and raised by Martians.
  The novel explores his interaction with—and eventual transformation of—terrestrial
  culture. The title is an allusion to the phrase in Exodus 2:22. According to Heinlein,
  the novel's working title was The Heretic. Several later editions of the book have
  promoted it as "The most famous Science Fiction Novel ever written".
