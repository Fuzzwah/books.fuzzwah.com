  York Times bestselling author of A Walk in the Woods and The Body takes his ultimate

  answer. “Brims with strange and amazing facts . . . destined to become a modern
  classic of science writing.”—The New York Times In A Walk in the Woods, Bill Bryson

  them with questions, apprenticed himself to their powerful minds. A Short History
