  have convinced some of the world''s greatest magicians to allow scientists to study
  their techniques for tricking the brain. This book is the result of the authors''

  that are hackable—a good magician uses your mind''s own intrinsic properties against
  you in a form of mental jujitsu. Now magic can reveal how our brains work in everyday

  magic and the mind.'
