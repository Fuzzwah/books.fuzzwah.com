  unique traveler's guide, you'll learn about 128 destinations around the world where

  swinging in Paris, ponder a descendant of Newton's apple tree at Trinity College,
  Cambridge, and more. Each site in The Geek Atlas focuses on discoveries or inventions,

  photos and illustrations, the book is organized geographically by country (by state
  within the U.S.), complete with latitudes and longitudes for GPS devices. - Publisher.
