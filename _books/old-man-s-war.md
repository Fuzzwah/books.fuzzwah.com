  \ willing to fight us for them are common. So: we fight. To defend Earth, and to\
  \ stake our own claim to planetary real estate. Far from Earth, the war has been\
  \ going on for decades: brutal, bloody, unyielding. Earth itself is a backwater.\

  \ Everybody knows that when you reach retirement age, you can join the CDF. They\

  \ serve two years at the front. And if you survive, you’ll be given a generous homestead\
